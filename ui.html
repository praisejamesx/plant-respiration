What this cannot see The response is a mixed gas signal. Water stress, light, temperature, handling, a cleaning product, a person, and sensor drift all move it. It cannot separate those causes, cannot identify a VOC, and has no channel that turns a response into an ethylene, CO₂, pest, or pathogen claim. Inspect the plant; treat a change as a reason to look, not a diagnosis.
